# Privacy Policy — Dot Weaver

**Last updated: 22 June 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game **Dot Weaver** ("the App", "we",
"us") handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to this policy.

## Who we are
Dot Weaver is a puzzle game. Contact: **sovedigital@gmail.com**.

## Information we collect
We do **not** ask you to create an account and we do **not** collect your name,
email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

We process the following:

- **Device identifier** — a per-device identifier (Android: ANDROID_ID; iOS:
  identifierForVendor). We send it to our server to power the online leaderboard,
  to restore your "Remove Ads" / premium status across launches, and to prevent abuse.
  You can view this identifier in the App under **Settings → Account → Device ID**.
- **Gameplay data** — your total stars and number of levels completed, sent to our
  server so the leaderboard can rank players.
- **Username** — when you first go online the App assigns a random nickname (e.g.
  "Weaver1234") that you can change in Settings. It is shown publicly on the
  leaderboard. It is a pseudonym and is not linked to your real identity.
- **Purchase status** — whether you purchased "Remove Ads". Payments are processed
  by Apple or Google; **we never receive your payment card or billing details.**
- **Advertising data (via Google AdMob)** — to show ads, Google AdMob may collect a
  mobile advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID), device information and app-usage data,
  and may use them for personalized advertising. See Google's policy below.
- **On-device data** — your progress, sound and language settings are stored locally
  on your device and are not transmitted to us.

## How we use information
- To run the global leaderboard and show your rank and nickname.
- To grant and restore ad-free / premium status.
- To display and measure advertising.
- To operate, maintain, secure and improve the App.

## Third parties
- **Google AdMob** (advertising): https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Apple App Store / Google Play** (in-app purchases and payment processing).
- On iOS, the App uses **App Tracking Transparency**: ads are personalized only if
  you grant permission in the system prompt; you can change this in iOS Settings.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Data retention and deletion
We keep leaderboard data while the App is in use. You may request deletion of your
server-side data (leaderboard entry, username, entitlement) at any time by emailing
**sovedigital@gmail.com** with your **Device ID** (found in Settings → Account).
We will delete it within 30 days.

## Advertising choices
- iOS: control tracking via the App Tracking Transparency prompt and **Settings →
  Privacy & Security → Tracking**.
- Android: reset or limit your advertising ID in **Settings → Google → Ads**.

## Security
We use industry-standard measures (including encrypted HTTPS connections) to protect
data in transit. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

## Children's privacy
Dot Weaver is a general-audience game and is **not directed to children** under 13
(or under the applicable age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us
and we will delete it.

## International transfers
Our servers may be located outside your country. By using the App you consent to the
transfer and processing of data as described here.

## Your rights
Depending on your region (e.g. GDPR, CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct or
delete your data, and to opt out of personalized ads. To exercise them, contact us at
**sovedigital@gmail.com**.

## Changes
We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated"
date above.
