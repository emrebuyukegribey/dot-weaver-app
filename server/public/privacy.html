Dot Weaver — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Dot Weaver ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Who we are

Dot Weaver is a puzzle game. Contact: sovedigital@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. We process:

  • Device identifier — a per-device identifier (Android: ANDROID_ID; iOS: identifierForVendor). We send it to our server to power the online leaderboard, to restore your "Remove Ads"/premium status across launches, and to prevent abuse. You can view it in Settings → Account → Device ID.
  • Gameplay data — your total stars and number of levels completed, used to rank players on the leaderboard.
  • Username — a random nickname (e.g. "Weaver1234") assigned when you first go online, editable in Settings, shown publicly on the leaderboard. It is a pseudonym not linked to your real identity.
  • Purchase status — whether you purchased "Remove Ads". Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we never receive your payment or billing details.
  • Advertising data (via Google AdMob) — Google AdMob may collect a mobile advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID), device information and app-usage data, and may use them for personalized advertising.
  • On-device data — progress, sound and language settings are stored locally and not transmitted to us.

How we use information

  • Run the leaderboard and show your rank and nickname.
  • Grant and restore ad-free/premium status.
  • Display and measure advertising.
  • Operate, maintain, secure and improve the App.

Third parties

  • Google AdMob: ads, privacy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play (in-app purchases and payment processing).
  • On iOS the App uses App Tracking Transparency: ads are personalized only if you grant permission; change it anytime in iOS Settings.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention and deletion

We keep leaderboard data while the App is in use. To delete your server-side data (leaderboard entry, username, entitlement), email sovedigital@gmail.com with your Device ID (Settings → Account). We will delete it within 30 days.

Advertising choices

iOS: App Tracking Transparency and Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Android: reset/limit your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.

Security

We use industry-standard measures (including encrypted HTTPS) to protect data in transit. No method is 100% secure.

Children's privacy

Dot Weaver is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International transfers

Our servers may be located outside your country. By using the App you consent to the transfer and processing of data as described here.

Your rights

Depending on your region (e.g. GDPR, CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data and to opt out of personalized ads. Contact sovedigital@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes are reflected by the "Last updated" date.

Contact

sovedigital@gmail.com
